1. Project Overview

StudySprint is an AI-assisted web application engineered to enhance student academic productivity through structured note management, automated practice quiz generation, interactive self-assessment, and personalized study session scheduling.

Core features for this increment: Subject, Topic & Notes Management (FR-002), AI Quiz Generation & Review (FR-003), Interactive Quiz Taking & Auto-Scoring (FR-004), and Admin Content Moderation (FR-010), plus Additional Features: Study Session Scheduling (FR-005), Progress Analytics Dashboard (FR-008), and Collaborative Quiz Sharing (FR-009).

User Roles and Access

Role

What this role can do in this increment

Student

Create, edit, and delete own Subjects, Topics, and Notes; generate, edit, and save 10-question AI Quizzes; take quizzes and view attempt history; schedule and complete study sessions; share quizzes and flag inappropriate shared quizzes; view live progress analytics.

Admin

View system-wide user and moderation metrics on /admin/dashboard; review all shared and flagged quizzes on /admin/moderation; dismiss flags or delete inappropriate quizzes; manage user account statuses.

User Flow

  1. Notes & AI Quiz Flow (FR-002, FR-003): Student opens /student/notes → Creates Subject and Topic → Pastes or uploads digital text notes → Clicks "Generate AI Quiz" → Reviews/edits generated 10-question draft → Clicks "Save Quiz & Start Practicing".
  2. Quiz Taking & Analytics Flow (FR-004, FR-008): Student selects answers on /student/quizzes/:id → Submits quiz → Receives immediate score, correct/incorrect feedback, and explanations → Attempt saved in quiz_attempts and reflected on /student/dashboard.
  3. Scheduling, Sharing & Moderation Flow (FR-005, FR-009, FR-010): Student schedules study sessions on /student/schedule and shares quizzes via /shared-quiz/:shareToken → Classmates can attempt or flag shared quizzes → Admin reviews flagged items on /admin/moderation and dismisses or removes them.

Validation and Business Rules

Rule

How it is enforced

Note Content Minimum Length

Requires ≥20 chars to save a note and ≥40 chars to generate a 10-question AI quiz (HTTP 400).

Resource Ownership & RBAC

All subject/topic/note/session/quiz queries filter by req.user.id; Admin moderation routes enforce authorize('admin') (HTTP 403).

Quiz Structure Validation

Verifies non-empty title, at least 1 question, ≥2 options per question, and valid correctIndex inside a SQLite transaction.

Session Duration Bounds

Study session duration validated between 10 and 480 minutes (HTTP 400).
